Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 3, 2026

At Partner Community (Sangath Partner), we respect your right to control and permanently delete your personal information. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You can request full account and data deletion at any time by contacting our support team via email:

⚠️ Important: User ID & Instagram Profile ID Required

To process your account deletion, you must provide both your User ID (first 12 characters, visible on your Profile screen in the app) and your Instagram Profile ID (Handle). Our team verifies and permanently deletes your account and all associated profile data based on these identifiers. Requests missing either identifier cannot be processed.

Email Request Details

  • Email Address: kajalkarkar007@gmail.com
  • Subject Line: Account Deletion Request - Partner Community
  • Information to Include:
    • User ID (First 12 characters, Required): Visible directly under your Instagram handle on your Profile screen in the app (e.g. User ID: a1b2c3d4e5f6).
    • Instagram Profile ID (Required): Your exact Instagram handle (e.g. @username) linked to your profile.
    • Registered Phone Number (Optional): For additional verification.

Once your email with your User ID and Instagram Profile ID is received, our admin team will locate your profile and permanently purge your account, photos, match history, and authentication records within 48 hours.

What Data is Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently wiped from our production database (Supabase & Firebase):

  • Authentication Record: Your phone number and login credentials in Firebase Auth.
  • Profile Data: Display name, age, city, bio, gender, and interests in Supabase PostgreSQL.
  • Profile Photos: All uploaded photos removed from cloud storage.
  • Connections & Requests: All sent and received partner requests, active matches, and unlocked contact handles.
  • Blocked/Reported List: Any personal blocklists or moderation associations.

Data Retention Policy

We do not retain any personal information after deletion. Aggregated, non-identifiable crash analytics (e.g. system stability metrics) may remain solely for technical diagnostic purposes.

Questions?

If you have any questions regarding data deletion, please email us at kajalkarkar007@gmail.com.
