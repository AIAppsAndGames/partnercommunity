Child Safety Standards & CSAE Prevention Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026 • Strict 18+ Platform Policy

This Child Safety Standards Policy applies directly to the mobile application Partner Community: Garba Meet (Application ID: app.partner.community), developed and published by AI Apps and Games.

We are committed to maintaining an uncompromising zero-tolerance platform against Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE). We strictly comply with Google Play Developer Policies (Child Safety Standards), international laws, and applicable local child protection regulations.

1. Strict 18+ Age Restriction & Gatekeeper

Partner Community: Garba Meet is designed and operated exclusively for adults aged 18 years and older seeking festive, cultural, and event dance companions (such as Navratri Garba partners).

  • Zero Minors Allowed: Minors under the age of 18 are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, browsing profiles, sending requests, or utilizing any feature of the application.
  • Age Verification Gatekeeper: During onboarding and account registration, users are explicitly prompted for their date of birth/age. Users who indicate they are under 18 years of age are immediately blocked from registration.
  • Account Termination: Any account identified or reported to belong to an individual under 18 years of age is permanently terminated immediately, and all associated profile data is permanently purged.

2. Zero Tolerance for CSAM & CSAE

We enforce an absolute, non-negotiable zero-tolerance policy against:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any visual, textual, or digital depiction of child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE): Child grooming, solicitation of minors, sextortion, child trafficking, sexualization of minors, or attempting to arrange real-world contact with a minor for sexual or exploitative purposes.
  • Inappropriate or Harmful Media: Any attempt to upload, transmit, store, display, or share sexually suggestive or explicit content involving minors.

Any user who attempts to transmit, solicit, upload, or facilitate such content or conduct will have their account permanently banned on the spot, with zero possibility of reinstatement, and will be reported to international child safety authorities and law enforcement.

3. In-App User Feedback & Reporting Mechanisms (Without Leaving the App)

In accordance with Google Play Child Safety Standards, Partner Community: Garba Meet provides active, accessible, and functional in-app mechanisms that allow users to submit feedback, report safety concerns, and flag potential CSAM/CSAE violations directly inside the application without leaving the app:

  • Native In-App Profile Reporting: Every user profile card and details view features a prominent report action (🚩). Tapping this opens a native modal directly in the app where users can select "Child Safety / Underage Concern (CSAE/CSAM)", provide additional details, and submit the report immediately without launching any external browser or third-party email application.
  • In-App Feedback & Safety Center: Accessible directly from the Profile & Settings tabs (Profile → In-App Feedback & Safety), users can submit feedback, report suspicious behavior, or raise child safety questions through an internal form. Submissions are securely delivered straight to our moderation backend, and an in-app receipt confirmation is displayed instantly on-screen.
  • Immediate In-App Block: Users can instantly block any individual profile (🚫), immediately removing them from discovery feeds, requests, and all mutual interactions.

4. Moderation, Takedown & Evidence Preservation Procedures

When a child safety or CSAM/CSAE report is received:

  • Immediate Account Suspension: The reported user account is placed on immediate suspension pending review.
  • Content Removal within 24 Hours: Any flagged content is reviewed and taken down immediately upon actual knowledge.
  • Evidence Preservation: Relevant access logs, IP identifiers, and uploaded assets are cryptographically preserved in secure records to assist law enforcement investigations and regulatory disclosures.

5. Mandatory Reporting to Law Enforcement & NCMEC

We fulfill our legal and moral obligations to report all instances of known or suspected CSAM/CSAE to official child safety authorities:

  • NCMEC CyberTipline: In compliance with 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and global reporting standards, all detected or confirmed incidents of CSAM/CSAE are reported directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline.
  • Law Enforcement Cooperation: We cooperate fully with local, federal, and international law enforcement bodies (including Indian cybercrime reporting portals and authorities under the POCSO Act 2012) in criminal investigations concerning crimes against children.

6. Legal Compliance & International Standards

Our operations, development, and moderation workflows strictly adhere to:

  • Google Play Developer Program Policies — Child Safety Standards
  • Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012
  • Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules
  • Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) standards
