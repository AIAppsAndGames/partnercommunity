Child Safety Standards & Protection Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Strict 18+ Platform Policy

Strict 18+ Age Restriction & Zero Tolerance: Partner Community is an exclusive platform intended strictly for adults (aged 18 and older) to discover Garba and festive cultural dance partners. Minors (under the age of 18) are strictly prohibited from creating accounts or using the service. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).

1. Commitment to Child Protection & Safety

Partner Community is committed to upholding the highest standards of user safety and strictly prohibits any behavior, content, or interaction that exploits, abuses, or endangers children. In accordance with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy, global laws, and applicable regulations:

  • We strictly prohibit the upload, generation, transmission, facilitation, or solicitation of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE).
  • Any account found attempting to solicit, share, or engage in CSAM/CSAE will be permanently banned immediately and reported to relevant law enforcement agencies, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local police authorities.

2. Age Verification & Gatekeeping

To ensure a safe environment for all adults:

  • All users are required to confirm that they are at least 18 years of age during the onboarding process.
  • We do not market, design, or target our application or services to children.
  • If we become aware that an account has been registered by an individual under 18 years old, we take immediate action to terminate the account and permanently delete all associated data from our servers.

3. Content Moderation & Reporting Mechanisms

To ensure strict enforcement of our safety policies:

  • In-App Safety Alerts: Mandatory fraud and safety advisories are presented before partner requests are dispatched.
  • Direct Support & Reporting: Users can report suspicious profiles or policy violations at any time via in-app mechanisms or by directly emailing our dedicated safety point of contact.
  • Prompt Investigation: Reported profiles are reviewed swiftly, and non-compliant content or profiles are removed immediately.
