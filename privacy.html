Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Welcome to Partner Community (also referred to as "Sangath Partner", "we", "us", or "our"). We are dedicated to maintaining the privacy, security, and trust of our community across our Android and iOS applications.

1. Summary of Core Principles

  • Zero In-App Chat Tracking or Spam: We use an explicit double-opt-in matching system. We do not maintain in-app chat backlogs or track unrequested messages.
  • Phone Number Confidentiality: Your phone number is strictly used for secure OTP verification via Firebase Authentication and is never displayed publicly to other users.
  • Protected Social Handles: Instagram handles and contact points are locked behind database Row-Level Security (RLS) and shared only when both members mutually accept a match request.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third parties or advertising brokers.

2. Information We Collect

When you register and use Partner Community, we collect the following categories of information:

A. Account & Verification Data

  • Phone Number: Collected during sign-up for OTP verification via Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile Details: Your chosen display name, age/birthdate (to verify 18+ eligibility), gender, city/location preference, and bio.
  • Profile Photos: Images uploaded to showcase your profile to prospective festival and event companions.
  • Social Handles (Optional): Your Instagram username, protected and only unlocked upon mutual match confirmation.

B. Purchases & Subscription Data

  • Subscriptions & In-App Purchases: We use RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and unlock premium features. RevenueCat processes anonymous app user IDs, transaction identifiers, purchase history, and entitlement statuses. Payments are securely processed directly by Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not store or process your credit card or financial numbers.

C. Usage and Diagnostics Data

  • Interactions: Partner requests sent, received, and mutual matches formed.
  • Technical & Diagnostic Information: Device model, OS version, app version, crash logs, and performance metrics collected via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to maintain app stability and eliminate bugs.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

To deliver our services reliably and securely, we integrate with trusted third-party infrastructure providers:

Service Provider Data Handled Purpose
Firebase Authentication (Google) Phone number, OTP verification tokens Secure, spam-free account sign-in
Supabase Profile data, match requests, uploaded photos Encrypted database storage with Row-Level Security (RLS)
RevenueCat Anonymous User ID, purchase tokens, subscription & entitlement status In-app purchase validation and cross-platform subscription synchronization
Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase Store transaction tokens and digital receipts Payment processing and store billing
Firebase Analytics Aggregated, anonymized app usage events Understanding app performance and feature adoption
Firebase Crashlytics Crash logs, stack traces, device model, OS version Detecting and fixing app crashes and stability issues

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for:

  • Creating, authenticating, and managing your user profile.
  • Enabling companion discovery for festivals, events, and community activities.
  • Facilitating double-opt-in matching and securely sharing mutual handles.
  • Validating subscription entitlements and in-app purchase benefits via RevenueCat.
  • Enforcing our 18+ age requirement and community safety standards.
  • Troubleshooting technical errors, bugs, and performance bottlenecks.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement rigorous technical and administrative security measures to protect your personal data. All communications between the app and our backend use industry-standard HTTPS/TLS encryption. Profile data and contact handles stored in Supabase are governed by strict Row-Level Security (RLS) rules that prevent unauthorized database querying.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have complete control over your data. You may update your profile or permanently delete your account at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Navigate to Settings → Account → Delete Account within the app. All your profile information, photos, active requests, and match history will be immediately and permanently erased from our database.
  • Manual Request: You can also email us at kajalkarkar007@gmail.com with your registered phone number, and our team will process the deletion within 48 hours.

7. Children & Age Eligibility (18+)

Partner Community is strictly designed for individuals aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors under the age of 18. If we discover that a user is under 18, their account and all associated data will be immediately purged.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our app features or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
